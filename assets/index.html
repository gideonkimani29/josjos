Glass Supply and Installation Questions in Eldoret

What is the best glass for shower enclosures?

Tempered or toughened glass is the safest and most common choice for frameless shower cubicles and bathroom glass enclosures in Eldoret homes and apartments.

Do you install glass balustrades for balconies?

Yes. We install laminated and toughened glass balustrades and stair railings designed for safety, durability, and modern architectural appeal.
